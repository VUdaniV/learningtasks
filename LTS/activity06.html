Steps in making Spam and Egg

  1. Slice the Spam Remove the meat from the can. Slice it into 1/4-inch thick slabs or cut it into small, bite-sized cubes.
  2. Prep the EggsCrack your eggs into a small bowl. Whisk them thoroughly if you want scrambled eggs, or keep them whole if you prefer sunny-side up.
  3. Sear the SpamPlace a non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the Spam slices or cubes directly to the dry pan.Cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side until gold, crispy, and the edges are lightly bronzed.
  4. Cook the EggsMove the crispy Spam to the side of the pan or transfer it to a plate. Lower the heat slightly and pour your whisked eggs (or crack the whole eggs) directly into the rendered Spam fat left in the pan. Gently stir for soft-scrambled eggs, or fry until the egg whites are fully set.
  5. Assemble and ServeTurn off the stove heat just before the eggs fully dry out to keep them fluffy. Top with a crack of black pepper or chopped green onions if desired. Serve immediately next to a hot scoop of white rice or between toasted bread slices for a quick breakfast sandwich.

Steps in making Eggplant

  1. Prep and DiceWash the eggplant and slice off the green stem. Cut it into even 1-inch cubes or 1/2-inch thick rounds, keeping the skin on for texture and nutrition.
  2. Salt to Draw Out Moisture Toss the eggplant pieces with 1 teaspoon of salt in a colander. Let it sit for 20 minutes to sweat out bitter juices, then rinse with cold water and pat completely dry with paper towels.
  3. Season GenerouslyPreheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Place the eggplant on a baking sheet, drizzle heavily with olive oil, and toss with garlic powder and black pepper until every piece is coated.
  4. Roast Until TenderSpread the pieces out in a single layer so they do not steam. Roast for 20 to 25 minutes, tossing halfway through, until the skins wrinkle and the flesh turns golden brown.
  5. Serve or IncorporateRemove from the oven and serve immediately as a side dish. Alternatively, toss the roasted cubes straight into a warm marinara pasta sauce or blend them into a creamy dip.

Steps in making Egg silog

  1. Fry the ProteinChoose your favorite breakfast protein—such as cured pork (tocino), cured beef (tapa), fried sausage (longganisa), or even spam. Heat a tablespoon of cooking oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the protein and cook until it is caramelized or browned to your liking. Remove it from the pan and set aside.
  2. Cook the Garlic Fried Rice (Sinangag)Leftover, day-old rice works best for fried rice because it is firmer and doesn't clump. Using the same pan, add a bit more oil if needed. Sauté 3-4 cloves of minced garlic until golden brown and aromatic. Add the cold, leftover rice and break up any clumps with a spatula.Toss and stir-fry everything together for 3-5 minutes until heated through. Season with salt and a dash of ground black pepper. Set the rice aside on a serving plate.
  3. Fry the Egg (Itlog)The classic and most popular way to serve a silog egg is sunny-side up, so the runny yolk mixes perfectly with the savory rice. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a clean pan over medium heat. Crack your egg carefully into the pan.Cook until the egg whites are completely opaque but the yolk is still soft and jiggly.Alternatively, for a professional touch, use a spoon to scoop hot oil from the pan and baste the egg whites until they are set but the yolk remains runny.
